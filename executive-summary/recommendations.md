# Recommendations: Margin Decline Diagnosis

## Summary

Revenue grew about 20% year over year, but Q4 margin fell from 16.2% (2016) to 9.8% (2017), and Q4 profit fell by $10,691. Yearly margin only slipped from 13.4% to 12.7%, so the problem was hidden. Two sub-categories explain most of the drop: Binders (69% of the total negative drag) and Tables (16%).

## Recommendation 1: Cap Discounts on Binders

Binders went from the most profitable Office Supplies sub-category (+$10,489 Q4 profit) to a net loss (-$2,199). Orders with an 80% discount doubled from 13 to 26, all in the Central region, and Central accounts for 79% of the Binders decline.

Fix: cap Binders discounts at 70% and require approval for anything higher.

Ownership: Central region sales management, with the discount approval policy.

## Recommendation 2: Rethink Tables' Pricing in the South

Tables loses money on most sales. Q4 2017 Tables orders rose 55% (33 to 51), and the South region accounts for 94% of the Tables decline.

Fix: review Tables' cost and pricing, and slow down South region sales until it is profitable.

Ownership: Furniture category pricing team, with a focus on the South.

## Recommendation 3: Track Margin Every Quarter, Not Just Yearly

Looking only at yearly numbers, the dip looked small while the real drop sat inside one quarter.

Fix: review margin by product and region every quarter, including how often maximum discounts are used, so a problem like this is caught in weeks, not a year.

Ownership: Finance and Sales Operations, as part of quarterly business reviews.
